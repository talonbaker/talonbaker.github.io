Integrated state of a bicycle. 8 generalized coordinates (Pitch is the dependent coordinate, kept consistent with the front-wheel-on-ground holonomic constraint by the integrator) plus the 3 independent generalized speeds (RollRate, SteerRate, RearWheelSpinRate).

Bit-blittable POD: MemoryMarshal.AsBytes(ref state) is safe.

UprightStationary(in BicycleParameters)

Bicycle at rest, upright, no steer, located at world origin. In Moore's generalized-coordinate convention (used by the generated EOM), the chain is A(yaw) -> B(roll q4) -> C(pitch q5 about B['2']). With both wheels on the ground, zero roll and zero steer, the rear-frame pitch q5 equals the steer-axis tilt lambda (the rear-frame body axis C['1'] is horizontal, so the rotation needed to bring B['1'] (also horizontal) to match it is exactly lambda). See PyDy's bicycle_validation example and Moore (2012) chapter 2 for the geometric derivation.
