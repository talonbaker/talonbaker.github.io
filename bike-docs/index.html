Bike — Whipple bicycle simulator + Unity game

A multiplayer bicycle game where the physics is honest and the game is hard.

This repository builds a tiny multiplayer game in the spirit of Flappy Bird, Getting Over It, and Baby Steps — but on a bike. The bike's balance comes from real bicycle dynamics, not Unity's stock rigidbody simulator. The difficulty comes from the rider's coordination problem: stay upright, pedal in rhythm, don't fall.

The physics is the canonical Whipple bicycle model — the same one used in the Meijaard et al. 2007 benchmark paper and Moore's 2012 PhD thesis on bicycle dynamics. The implementation matches published eigenvalues to six decimal places.

Two libraries, one game

Library Purpose
Bicycle.Physics Deterministic, allocation-free C# physics core. Integrates the full nonlinear Whipple EOM at 250 Hz via RK4.
Bicycle.World N-bike orchestrator. Wraps the physics library behind a clean event-driven contract consumed by Unity.

The Unity layer (unity-v2/) sits on top of Bicycle.World and handles rendering, input, and scene lifecycle.
