MEMO: RE: THE GAME

You are the ghost in the fluorescent lights.

Simmili drops you into a cutaway early-2000s office building as a silent observer — a cruel-but-fond god watching 15 intertwined NPCs fumble through their workdays. You don't command them. They drive themselves, pushed by hunger, loneliness, office politics, and the slow decay of everything around them.

Your only lever is spatial arrangement. Move a desk. Reassign a room. Put the passive-aggressive accountant three feet from the microwave-fish guy. Then sit back and watch the emergent chaos cascade through the entire floor.

Without intervention, things fall apart. With it, they fall apart differently — and sometimes, beautifully.

QUARTERLY FEATURE REVIEW

What Makes the Office Tick

Autonomous NPCs

15 characters driven by discomfort — hunger, social need, rest, work pressure, romance. They act on their own thresholds, not your orders.

Spatial Management

Your primary verb is arrangement. Seating charts, room assignments, proximity. Moving someone 10 feet reshapes relationships, routines, and outcomes.

Emergent Cascades

Bad chili → bathroom emergency → public disruption → relationship damage. Systems interlock. Stories write themselves. You just watch.

Modding API

A clean public API surface for the community. Add new behaviors, systems, and scenarios. The crown jewel of the post-launch roadmap.
