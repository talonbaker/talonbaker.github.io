Card-Fling Prototypes

A run of LÖVE2D look-dev and gameplay prototypes exploring a single idea: flinging cards. Compiled to WebAssembly via love.js and running in your browser, unmodified from the native builds.

CardFlingGame 3D · MIRROR

The fling reimagined as dealing. Flick a card forward across a table — it arcs, falls, lands and slides, and where you grabbed it decides how it spins. A browser mirror of the Godot build's math, with live sliders for the camera and every flight number.

Play →

Shiny

Lit finishes look-dev: fire, foil, glass, ice, wax, and gold materials reacting to a real light source. Glass truly refracts the backdrop; fire's flicker spills onto foil. Press 1–4 to switch scenes.

Play →

Goblin

Possibilist, prototype #0 — a hand of cards fanned out like poker. Drag up to throw a card at the goblin, drag sideways to reorder your hand. R restarts, ESC quits.

Play →

Wheel

Probabilist, proto #1 — the press-your-luck pot. Fling a card into the half-buried wheel; where it lands stamps its finish and pays into an unbanked pot you can bust. Bank with B/Enter.

Play →

Swinging Light

A warm lamp swinging on a rope and cool morning window light, each casting real, independently-computed shadows in a 2.5D isometric diorama. Toggle the lights with 1/2.

Play →
Running in "compatibility mode" (no SharedArrayBuffer / pthreads) so it works without special server headers — audio may be a little rough as a result. Best in a recent desktop Chrome or Firefox; click the canvas once to let audio start. These are throwaway prototypes exploring feel, not finished games.
